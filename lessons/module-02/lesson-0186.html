Pause before revealing

What must happen first, and what useful option disappears if you reverse the order?

Bb5++ opens the e-file and checks on the diagonal simultaneously. The forcing element must come before the opponent can repair the position.

The idea, made simple

In double check, two pieces attack the king at once. The king must move; capture and interposition cannot stop both lines.

When two moves look related, test A-then-B and B-then-A. One version often allows an escape, trade, or new defender.

Use this thinking sequence

  1. 1

    Write the intended final picture.

  2. 2

    Test Bb5++ first.

  3. 3

    Reverse the order mentally.

  4. 4

    Choose the sequence that removes the opponent’s choice.

The believable mistake: Calculating blocks or captures that answer only one of the two checks.
